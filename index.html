Privacy Policy — Quiz Italiano A2 B1

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Developer: Syed Wajahat Attari

Contact: syedwajahatattari@gmail.com

Quiz Italiano A2 B1 ("the App") helps you prepare for the Italian A2 language test (permesso di soggiorno) and the B1 language test (citizenship) with quizzes, study guides and exam simulations. This policy explains what data the App and its third-party services handle.

1. No account, no login

The App does not require an account, a login, or any personal information from you to use it. There is no profile, no username, and no password.

2. Data stored on your device only

Your learning progress — quiz results, error bank, per-track (A2 / B1) progress, app language and theme — is stored locally on your device (SharedPreferences / local storage). It never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. We do not receive, store or see any of it.

Optional speaking recordings are saved as audio files in the App’s documents directory on your device. The App does not upload recordings or send them to RevenueCat or any other service. Android app backup is disabled; the iOS recordings directory is excluded from backup. Clear the App’s storage or uninstall it to delete recordings.

3. Advertising in the free version

The free version of the App shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device identifiers to serve ads. PRO users see no ads.

4. Purchases — PRO (Google Play and RevenueCat)

PRO is sold as an in-app purchase / subscription through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google; we never see or store your card number or bank details.

To verify your PRO status the App uses RevenueCat. When you buy or restore PRO, RevenueCat receives:

  • a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID,
  • your purchase history for this App (product, purchase date, expiry date),
  • your device type and App version.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or payment details. Its privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Learning content and on-device speech

  • Questions and guides are bundled inside the App and work offline. The App does not send your questions or answers to any server or AI service.
  • Italian pronunciation (Ascolto practice) uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (flutter_tts). Speech is generated on your device; no audio, text, question or answer is sent to any server.
  • Speaking practice: microphone permission is requested when you choose to record. You can stop recording, play the recording locally, and compare it with a bundled model answer. Recording is optional; you can deny microphone permission or revoke it in system settings.
  • Fonts: the App downloads fonts from Google Fonts at runtime. Google receives a normal web request (including your IP address) for the font files.

6. What we do NOT collect

  • No location tracking (the App never asks for your location).
  • No contacts, photos, or camera access. Microphone access is used only for optional speaking recordings on your device.
  • No analytics or usage tracking SDKs.
  • PRO users see no ads; advertising data in the free version is described in section 3.
  • No data is sent to any AI service.
  • No data is sold to anyone, ever.

7. Children

The App is a language-test study aid for adults preparing for the A2 or B1 Italian language test. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your choices

  • Delete local data: clear the App's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App.
  • Purchases: manage or cancel a PRO subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Questions or requests about this policy: email syedwajahatattari@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
